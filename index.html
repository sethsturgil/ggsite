The Timeline of Chaos

August 29, 1956
Born "Jesus Christ Allin" in Lancaster, New Hampshire. Raised in a rural cabin by an abusive, fanatical father. His brother Merle Jr. couldn't pronounce Jesus, giving him the nickname "GG".

1977 – 1984
Starts fronting The Jabbers, playing raw, melodic power-pop influenced punk rock. Releases his debut album.

Late 1980s
Shifts into extreme shock-rock behavior. His live performances become infamous for severe self-mutilation, violence, and dynamic crowd confrontations.

1989 – 1993
Spends significant time in prison for felony assault. Appears on mainstream daytime television talk shows like Geraldo and Jerry Springer while briefly sober.

June 27 – 28, 1993
Plays his final, chaotic show at The Gas Station in NYC. Passes away from an accidental heroin overdose the following morning at age 36.

Infamous Live Shows & Gig History

1980 – Cantones (Boston, MA)
Early era shows performing melodic punk alongside The Jabbers. Fast-paced, high energy, but before the extreme shock antics began.
1985 – Peoria Theater (Peoria, IL)
Widely cited as the first show where his extreme, modern "shock-rock" performance methods completely manifested, changing his live dynamic forever.
1988 – L'Amour (Brooklyn, NY)
A highly volatile, chaotic performance that was heavily bootlegged on VHS tapes throughout the underground tape-trading circles of the late 80s.
1991 – Space Fish Lounge (Orlando, FL)
An infamous concert with The Murder Junkies that ended in a massive venue riot and resulted in another local police arrest warrant.
June 27, 1993 – The Gas Station (New York City, NY)
The final performance. After the power was cut a few songs into the set, he led a massive crowd of punks on a walking riot through Manhattan's East Village just hours before his death.
